Clearbook Lite

A phone companion for advice-only planners. See the whole client book, catch what needs attention, and clear suggestions between meetings. The full desktop app still does the deep work.

  • Book: every household, review status and what is waiting
  • Household: allocation against target with drift bands, tax exposure and account placement
  • Review: one suggestion at a time, with the reason and the numbers
  • Alerts: wash-sale windows, drift breaches, expired fee waivers, stale data

Or tap the round ? button in the app. The walkthrough points at each part and waits for your taps; it never taps for you.

All households and figures are made-up sample data. The planner’s rules make every suggestion and the planner signs off on each one.
